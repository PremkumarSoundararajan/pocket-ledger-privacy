1. Overview

Pocket Ledger ("the App") is a personal expense tracking application. Your privacy is critically important to us. This policy outlines how your data is handled when you use Pocket Ledger.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Pocket Ledger accesses Google Sheets via Google OAuth 2.0 strictly to record and manage your personal expense logs.

We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell your personal data, login credentials, or spreadsheet records to any external servers or third parties.

All expense data is written directly to your private Google Drive account.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses official Google APIs (Google OAuth 2.0 and Google Sheets API) solely for core functionality. No advertising SDKs, tracking libraries, or analytics services are integrated into the App.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data resides within your own Google Account, you retain total ownership. You can delete your expense data at any time by editing or deleting the spreadsheet in Google Drive, or by revoking Pocket Ledger's access via your Google Account Permissions.
